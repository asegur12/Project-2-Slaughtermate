This is a spec for the second project of the final project

The problem: The client is bored and wants a game generated for them, however, they want a game generated for them that they can play, but they want one on a different style. This client played two different games in the past time of days, those games being
	-Chess, an ancient game of strategy, it should be known
	-A hack'n'slash game where the player gains various powers and weapons to slaughter waves of enemies and bosses

The client is assumed to want a game that is tailored to combine both of these elements, with chess being the design and the hack n slash being the actual gameplay, they desire a game combining both where there should be:
	-A large map, with some structures like cities or castles
	-Enemies, a large swarm that come towards the player from all sides and spawn on the outskirts of the map
	-Powerful weapons and spells, ones that can deal large amounts of damage and have large areas of effect or range, specifically the high tier should have something to do with sun, moon, darkness, light, tech and hyper types
	-Difficulty and rewards, enemies should scale in hp and strength with each level gained and rewards should be random

The client has also listed a few suggestions that should be directly added, because they really want it:
	-6 bosses, a big giant piece of each kind that concludes each wave and needs to be killed before going to the next one
	-Really large spells, high tier spells that are able to deal large amounts of damage in a large area, and a variety of other spells too
	-Cool weapons, each with their own class
	-a shop where I can buy spells and other items
From these suggestion, the clients game should have the following aswell:
	-A rarity system of spells, 5 tiers, tier 1 to 5, with 1 and 2 being small scale spells that can clear out lower rank enemies quickly, struggle with higher rank enemies, but can deal sufficient damage. 3 and 4 being average to strong spells that can deal large scale damage, in larger areas, and tier 5, spells that deal massive damage and effect equally massive areas
	-a decent spell loadout, atleast 5 to 8 spells of each type, to maintain space, and have the user be able to equip 3 spells
	-Enemy scaling, as said before, but they should be able to survive powerful attacks and kill the user by the late game
	-fair randomness system, make it so each reward that gives spells maintain a fair way to get each type
	-Weapons class for each run, from what the client wants, there should be 4 weapon classes:
		-Sword: Melee slashes and a spin slash for a charged attack
		-Bow: Ranged attack and a big arrow for a charged attack
		-Staff: Ranged area attacks and a big magic blast as a charged attack
		-Hammer: Melee area attacks and a giant slam as a charged attack

From these suggestions, you, the agent who will generate this program as listed and described above.
