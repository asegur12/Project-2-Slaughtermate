# Project-2-Slaughtermate: Magic Rush
The second of 3 projects generated by Claude AI for my CSC299 class: a simple hack'n'slash game where you slaughter waves of enemies. **Since Round 4 the game is called Magic Rush** and has a fantasy medieval look. The original chess look (Slaughtermate) is still available as a theme on the start screen.

## How to play
**Windows app:** run `dist\MagicRush.exe`. It opens the game in its own window (no browser tabs or address bar) and saves your Hall of Heroes scores between runs. Scores saved by the old `Slaughtermate.exe` carry over. To rebuild the .exe after changing the game, run:

```powershell
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File desktop\build.ps1
```

**Browser:** double-click `index.html` to open it in any modern browser. It runs fully offline: no install, no internet.

| Control | Action |
|---|---|
| W A S D | Move |
| Space | Dash toward the cursor (1 s cooldown) |
| Left click | Basic attack (aimed at the mouse) |
| Hold left click, release | Charged attack |
| 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 | Cast equipped spells |
| Esc / P | Pause (Esc also closes the merchants' cart) |

## The game
- **Two themes.** The fantasy theme is the default. The **♟ Switch to the chess theme** button on the start screen brings back the Round 3 chess look: pieces, boards, the Slaughtermate name and fragments. Gameplay is identical in both themes, and the game remembers your choice.
- **6 floors, each a new map:** The Great Plains (grass, trees and gnome villages), The Old Village (wooden houses, wells and hay), The Magic School (an azure library of bookshelves and study halls), The Dragon Ruins (broken stone, ruins and glowing gems), The Royal Witch Swamp (marsh pools, witch huts and witch towers that shoot at you) and The Evil King's Castle (black, gold and red halls with pillars and statues). Survive the swarm until the floor's boss arrives, then kill it.
- **Difficulty.** Pick Easy, Normal or Hard on the menu. It scales enemy HP, damage and projectile speed, and multiplies your score (×0.75 / ×1 / ×1.5).
- **Your hero** depends on your weapon: a **knight** (Sword, Hammer, Shield, Club, Scythe), a **wizard** (Staff, Tome) or an **archer** (Bow). After each boss you rise in rank (for example Squire → Knight → Knight-Captain → Paladin → Lord Paladin → Champion). Each rank grants bonuses and adds something to your look: a cape, a plume, gold trim, pauldrons and finally a crown.
- **Enemies:** goblins swarm, skeletons hop in L-shapes (over walls), apprentice mages move diagonally and snipe, minotaurs charge in straight lines, witches fire volleys and lunge, and royal guards summon goblins and rally nearby allies. Fallen enemies tip over and vanish in a puff of smoke.
- **Bosses:** Goblin Chief, Evil Prince, Red Mage, Red Dragon, Witch Queen and the Tyrant King. Each has its own telegraphed attacks, enrages below 50% HP and goes out in a large burst.
- **8 weapons** (pick 1 per run): Sword (Spin Slash), Bow (Great Arrow), Staff (Arcane Blast), Hammer (Earth Slam), Scythe (semicircle sweeps; Crude Reap heals you), Shield (knockback bashes; Shield Rush), Club (rectangular slams; wind-up swing with 3 chain explosions) and Tome (3 piercing spirits that deal damage over time; 9-spirit burst).
- **75 spells in 5 tiers**, one of each of 15 elements per tier: fire, ice, lightning, earth, wind, nature, poison, arcane, light, sun, moon, darkness, tech, hyper and mind. Heavy tier 3–5 spells have a short wind-up. You can equip **5**, and picking a spell you already own levels it up. Each spell shows its element's icon: a flame, rocks, the sun, a crescent moon, a 4-pointed star, a hand, a power symbol, a snowflake, a tree, three shapes (hyper), a swirl, a tornado, a storm cloud, an eye, a skull (poison), and a crown for Grandmaster spells.
- **Every element looks different.** Fire burns with flames, sun spells are a deep red with jagged crescents, ice throws snowflakes and freezes the ground, poison leaves skulls and puddles, lightning crackles in grey storm clouds, arcane opens periwinkle portals, hyper scatters chess-piece outlines, tech draws grids and pixels, mind warps colour, moon brings a starry night sky, light streaks, darkness raises hands from a purple void, nature grows vines and tree spikes, wind rolls in clouds and fog, and earth throws rocks, cracks the ground and covers it in solid brown.
- **9 Grandmaster spells** rank above tier 5:
  - **Game of Despair:** two rings of phantom chess pieces feed a crown that shatters into a burst that weakens everything.
  - **Mental Inversion:** a vivid psychic pulse turns the swarm against itself.
  - **Grand Seal:** four crimson glyphs chain themselves to a great portal that drags enemies in, then it explodes and freezes them.
  - **Doom Desire:** marks every enemy on screen, then grey pillars smite them.
  - **Sweet Slumber:** a fairy vortex that explodes and puts enemies to sleep for 7 s.
  - **Fog of the Nightmare:** pitch-black fog around you where hands grab every enemy, and attackers outside get fog of their own.
  - **Artificial Calamity:** a reactor that slows everything, then explodes and turns survivors into goblins.
  - **Hellfire:** crimson fireballs sweep across and down the screen, leaving fire pits.
  - **Excalibur:** a giant sword falls, plants itself with a shockwave, then fires a huge beam of light.

  Grandmaster spells can appear as rewards from floor 4 on, and the merchants sell one.
- **Fair randomness.** Every level-up offers 3 cards. Each tier (Grandmaster too) uses a shuffle bag, so every spell in a tier shows up before any repeats, and pity counters raise the odds of tier 4–5 and Grandmaster cards the longer you go without one.
- **Merchants' cart.** After each boss on floors 1–5, a cart with three travelling merchants rolls into the centre of the map. Walk into it to shop for spell scrolls, relics (stat upgrades), a healing elixir, rerolls and, from floor 4, a Grandmaster spell. Close it (Esc or the advance button) to go on to the next floor. Enemies drop **gold bars**, which are the currency.
- **Spell logbook.** The menu has a logbook of all 84 spells. A spell's page unlocks once you obtain it in a run.
- **Crowns.** Win a run and that weapon gets a crown on the menu: small for Easy, medium for Normal, large for Hard.
- **Scaling.** Enemy HP and damage rise with each floor, your level and time spent on the floor.
- **Hall of Heroes.** The menu shows the top 10 runs (highest floor, score, weapon and difficulty), saved in your browser.

**Score** = (kills (enemy value × floor) + 2,500 × floor per boss + 1,000 per floor reached + 50 × level + 10,000 for a victory) × difficulty multiplier.
